03 / BEFORE YOU START

A little context.
A clearer first run.

What to bring, what gets saved, and how to interpret the result.

What do I need to try it?

A public HTTPS GET or HEAD endpoint. You can start with one URL, or import a browser HAR containing requests and captured responses. You choose what to replay.

Does importing a HAR send its requests?

No. Importing prepares an editable trace. Destination requests are sent only when you choose Replay. Read-only GET and HEAD are the supported methods.

How are credentials and history handled?

Your browser holds the key to a private workspace. Saved traces and run history live on the server. HAR credential fields are discarded or redacted; temporary authorization must be supplied again for a run. Review captured bodies before saving sensitive data.

What does a passing replay mean?

It means the observed response passed the configured checks and baseline comparison. It does not prove the entire application is correct. Public HTTPS destinations only; private networks and redirects are not supported.
